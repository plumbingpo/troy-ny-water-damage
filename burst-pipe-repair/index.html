Few water damage events are as common, or as potentially devastating to a property, as a pipe that bursts. At 4 to 8 gallons a minute, just one burst can send 240 to 480 gallons each hour into your ceilings, walls and flooring. Whether the cause is freezing temperatures, aging plumbing, excessive water pressure, corrosion or physical damage, a burst pipe demands immediate emergency response to limit the damage water does to structural materials, flooring, personal belongings and building systems.

When a pipe bursts in Troy or the surrounding area, Troy Restoration Co runs an emergency response around the clock. Our restoration technicians can help you locate and shut off your water supply, and we coordinate with licensed plumbers so the plumbing repair is done properly. As soon as the flow is shut down, extraction starts: portable and truck-mounted units pull standing water out of every affected area as fast as we can.

Following extraction, our structural drying process addresses all moisture-affected materials, including drywall, insulation, wood framing, subflooring and finished flooring. Burst pipes often damage multiple rooms and multiple levels, because water follows gravity through wall cavities, floor assemblies and ceiling spaces. Hidden moisture leads to mold and a deteriorating structure, so we use moisture detection equipment and thermal imaging to map the water's path and make sure all affected areas get dried.

Maintenance Tips for Burst Pipe Repair

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Monitor your water pressure, because excessive pressure (above 80 PSI) strains pipes and fittings
  • Going away in cold months? Keep the thermostat running to protect the plumbing from freezing
  • Every few years, ask a licensed plumber to look over the system for corrosion and worn parts
  • Install water leak detection sensors near water heaters and main supply lines
  • During freezing weather, keep faucets dripping and cabinet doors open so heat can circulate
  • Wrap any bare pipe that runs through a crawl space, garage or attic without heat

When to Call for Burst Pipe Repair in Troy

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • A stained, soggy or bowed section of drywall overhead or beside you
  • Damp, moldy odors around sinks, tubs or walls that hide pipes
  • An unusually high water bill that points to a hidden leak
  • Hissing or rushing noises in the walls with every tap turned off
  • A spray or steady drip coming off pipework you can see
  • Frost or ice on exposed pipes during cold weather
  • Tap water that turns brown, yellow or cloudy
