Dealing With Sump Pump Failure Cleanup in Troy

A sump pump has one job, and it tends to fail at the moment it is needed most. Groundwater rises in a big storm and pours into the pit, right as the electricity fails, the float jams or the inflow outruns the pump. The pit overflows and the basement floor starts to flood. Nothing stops it until some pump is working again.

Clean is the wrong word for what comes out of a sump pit. Soil, silt, bacteria and the contents of the drain tile travel with it, so our team rates it no better than Category 2, and Category 3 when it has stood for a while or sewage is involved. For the job, this means soaked porous items such as drywall and carpet pad are usually discarded, and what stays gets washed and disinfected first, then dried.

Do This First

What to Do Right Now

  1. Keep Out of the BasementWith the electricity still on, a flooded basement is not safe to walk into. Outlets, the pump cord or the furnace might already be wet and live.
  2. Shut Off Basement PowerTurn off the basement circuits at the breaker panel, but only if you can reach it from a dry spot. Call the utility when the panel itself sits in the flooded area.
  3. Check the Pump SafelySee if the plug pulled out, the outlet has tripped or the float got stuck on the side of the pit. Keep your hands out of the water.
  4. Assume the Water Is DirtyIf going down cannot be avoided, put on gloves and rubber boots, leave pets and children upstairs, and wash up after handling wet items.
  5. Move Valuables and CallTake pictures of the pump and the water level, bring valuables that are still dry upstairs, and phone us. Holding off until the basement empties is not the answer.
What We Do

How We Handle Sump Pump Failure Cleanup

1

Pump Out the Basement

Because the pumps and extraction units are ours, the broken sump does not hold up the work. We clear silt and standing water off the floor, out of low areas and from around the pit.

2

Take Out What Got Wet

Wearing protective gear, we take out the carpet, pad, wet drywall and insulation that the groundwater touched, bag it and haul it away so clean framing and concrete are exposed.

3

Clean and Sanitize

We wash the framing, the slab and the contents that are staying, then apply an antimicrobial. Doing it ahead of drying keeps the air movers from spreading contaminants through the basement.

4

Dry and Confirm

Typically for 3 to 5 days, air movers and dehumidifiers stay in place, until the concrete and framing read normal on a moisture meter. Before we pull our equipment, the pit should have a pump that works.

Insurance

Does Insurance Cover It?

Few water claims confuse homeowners as much as this one. Water backing up or overflowing from a sump is usually excluded under a standard homeowners policy, and a flood policy is a different product with rules of its own. Unless you bought an endorsement for sump overflow or water backup there is often no coverage, and such endorsements are capped. Your declarations page will list it, or your agent can tell you. We record the pump failure, the water level and the damage so your claim file is complete.

The Usual Culprits

What Causes It

  • An intake screen packed with mud and stones so the pump cannot draw water.
  • A float switch that is stuck, tangled or pinned against the basin wall.
  • Years of on and off cycling finally burning out an aging motor.
  • Undersized equipment that falls behind when inflow is heavy.
  • A power outage during the storm with no battery backup on the pump.
If It Is Left

What Happens if You Wait

Stored Belongings

Cardboard boxes, furniture legs, books and fabrics soak up contaminated water quickly. Within days mold shows up on absorbent things that were in it, and they are difficult to disinfect.

Finished Basement Walls

Drywall and insulation wick dirty water upward from the slab, well above the visible water line. The lower part that got wet is cut away, not dried, on account of the contamination.

Wall Framing

Framing lumber at floor level takes on groundwater before anything else in the wall. Once the drywall is off, the plates and studs are washed, disinfected and dried.

Basement Mechanical Equipment

Mechanical equipment often shares the floor with the sump pit. If wiring, burners or control boards got wet they may be ruined, and no appliance should be restarted until it has been checked.
