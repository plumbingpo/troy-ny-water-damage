A sewage backup is one of the most hazardous situations a property owner can face. Pathogens in raw sewage include salmonella, giardia, hepatitis and E. coli, along with other viruses, parasites and bacteria, and contact with anything contaminated is a serious risk to health. Troy Restoration Co provides emergency sewage cleanup throughout Troy, responding 24/7 with properly trained technicians, appropriate personal protective equipment and EPA-registered disinfectants to decontaminate your property safely.

Water damage comes in categories, and sewage sits in the worst one: Category 3, called black water, the highest level of contamination and danger. This classification requires the most stringent cleanup protocols, including full personal protective equipment, containment barriers, removal of all porous materials that contacted sewage and thorough disinfection of every remaining surface. Our technicians follow IICRC S500 standards for Category 3 water damage so your property is restored to safe occupancy standards.

Here is what the work covers: standing sewage is pumped out, contaminated water is extracted from the structure, porous materials the sewage touched (drywall, insulation, carpet and padding) are torn out and disposed of properly, hard surfaces are disinfected thoroughly, antimicrobials are applied, and the structure is dried so mold does not follow. We also deodorize the affected area with industrial-grade hydroxyl generators and thermal fogging equipment to eliminate lingering sewage odors.

Sewage Cleanup Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Every 2 to 3 years, get a sewer camera run through the line to spot developing issues
  • Route sump pump and gutter water away from the sewer line, which can back up when storm flow overloads it
  • Clear tree roots near sewer lines, which can infiltrate and block the pipes
  • Find your sewer cleanout now and check its condition, so it can be reached in an emergency
  • Wipes clog sewer lines even when the label says flushable, so only toilet paper goes down the toilet
  • Never pour grease, oil or food waste down your drains, since these cause blockages

How to Tell You Need Sewage Cleanup

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Lower levels or drain openings giving off a heavy sewage odor, sometimes like rotten eggs
  • Dark residue from sewage left on the basement floor or ringing a drain
  • Gurgling sounds from toilets or drains when other fixtures are used
  • Damp patches on a wall or floor that also smell foul
  • Several fixtures backing up at once instead of only one
  • A toilet overflow containing waste that has spread onto flooring or carpet
  • Sluggish drainage at all fixtures, a sign the main line is blocking up

Why Hire a Professional for Sewage Cleanup?

Sewage cleanup is not something any homeowner should attempt. Because raw sewage is a biological hazard, the work demands trained people, protective equipment (gloves, boots, respirators and Tyvek suits) and disinfection products registered with the EPA and not sold to the public. Hepatitis, gastrointestinal disease and infections of the skin or respiratory system are among the serious illnesses these pathogens cause.

Disposal matters as well, since everything the sewage contaminated has to be handled as biohazard waste. Putting carpet, insulation or drywall soaked with sewage out with household trash breaks the law and endangers sanitation workers and your community. Licensed biohazard disposal facilities take this waste from certified restoration firms, which keep the required protocols in place.
