Signs It Is Time for Storm Damage Repair

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Damp, earthy odors that appear a few days after the storm
  • Puddles near skylights, beside a chimney or on the attic floor
  • Wet insulation in the attic or wall cavities found after bad weather
  • Water getting past doors, windows and wall penetrations when wind pushes the rain
  • Damaged or displaced flashing around roof penetrations, valleys or edges
  • Flooding in lower levels because drainage systems were overwhelmed
  • Sagging or detached gutters that spill rainwater down the siding

The Benefits of Professional Storm Damage Repair

Because one kind of storm damage feeds another, the work needs restoration professionals with real experience. A roof leak that looks minor from inside may have done extensive hidden damage to insulation, roof decking and wall cavities, and that leads to mold growth and structural deterioration if it is not found and dried. Professional restoration technicians use thermal imaging and moisture meters to trace water paths and identify every affected area.

Emergency board-up and tarping also take proper technique to be effective. If a tarp is fastened poorly, it can direct water where you do not want it or give out when the wind picks up. We have done a lot of storm mitigation, and we use suitable materials and methods so the temporary protection holds until permanent repairs are on the calendar.

Maintenance Tips for Storm Damage Repair

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Take dated photos of the house every year, since insurers ask about prior condition
  • Extend downspouts and slope the soil so runoff moves away from the foundation
  • Make sure the attic is properly ventilated and insulated to prevent ice dams
  • Cut back limbs that hang over or close to the house so wind cannot throw them into it
  • Install impact-resistant windows or storm shutters if you live in a hurricane-prone area
  • Learn where the main water shutoff is and how to close it if pipes are damaged in a storm

Our systematic approach delivers quality results on every project. Here's how we handle storm damage repair from start to finish:

Emergency Board-Up & Tarping
