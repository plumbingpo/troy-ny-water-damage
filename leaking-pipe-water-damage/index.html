Leaking Pipe Water Damage in Troy: What to Know

You seldom catch a pipe leak the day it begins. The drip is hidden in a wall cavity, a sink cabinet or beneath the slab, so the first clue tends to be a stain, a spongy patch of floor or an oddly high water bill. Often that means weeks of moisture have already gone into whatever surrounds the pipe. We serve Troy homeowners by tracing the full wet area and drying every bit of it, including what is out of sight.

Fixing the pipe ends the leak, yet the moisture already inside the wall or floor stays put. That is our part of the job. With meters and a thermal camera we locate every wet material, make only the openings required, then keep drying equipment running until moisture levels read normal again. A slow leak gives mold plenty of time to begin, so we inspect for growth too and treat any surface it has reached.

Do This First

What to Do Right Now

  1. Shut Off the WaterClose the fixture's supply valve if the leak is at a sink or toilet. Not sure where it is coming from? Close the main shutoff for the whole house.
  2. Keep Power AwayTurn off the breaker serving the area when water is close to a light fixture, switch or outlet. Do not touch wet electrical devices or cords.
  3. Read the MeterShut off all taps and fixtures, then go look at the water meter. If the dial is still moving, a pipe is leaking somewhere you cannot see, possibly under the slab.
  4. Empty and PhotographClear out the cabinet or closet near the leak and take photos of stains, warped materials and the pipe itself before anything is repaired or thrown away.
  5. Have the Area MeteredBefore the opening is sealed, get the surrounding materials checked with a moisture meter. Inside the cavity, moisture can remain even when the wall surface feels dry.
Insurance

Does Insurance Cover It?

A slow leak and a sudden one are not handled the same way by insurance. Sudden and accidental damage is often covered, but damage from a leak that went on for weeks or months usually is not, and some policies exclude it by name. If the leak was concealed and you could not have known, it may be viewed more favorably than a visible leak nobody dealt with. Your agent and the policy itself are the places to confirm. So the adjuster can work from solid facts, we provide photos, moisture readings and notes on the leak source.

Common Causes

How It Usually Starts

  • High water pressure that stresses joints and valves year after year until one weeps.
  • Loose or worn compression fittings and supply connections under sinks and behind toilets.
  • Leaks at a sink drain joint or trap, which release a little water with every use.
  • Cold snaps that freeze piping in unheated spaces, leaving small cracks that leak after the thaw.
  • Pinhole corrosion in older copper lines that slowly eats through the pipe wall.
What Is at Risk

What Happens if You Wait

Stained Drywall and Ceilings

A pipe leaking above a ceiling or inside a wall leaves brown rings and bubbled paint. If the board keeps taking on water it loses its stiffness, starts to sag and may have to be removed.

Swollen Cabinets

Sink cabinet bottoms are usually particleboard, which swells and crumbles when it soaks up a leak. They do not return to shape, so long leaks often mean replacing the base.

Wet Insulation

Like a sponge, wall insulation takes in water from the pipe and keeps the framing next to it damp. Since a closed wall gives it almost no airflow, soaked insulation is normally removed.

Decayed Wall Framing

Wall studs and bottom plates that stay damp for weeks begin to soften and decay. Wood that has lost its strength has to be cut out and replaced.

Our Leaking Pipe Water Damage Process

Map the Moisture

We scan walls, floors and cabinets with moisture meters and a thermal camera to mark every wet area, including spots well away from the pipe where water has traveled along framing.

2

Small, planned openings give us access to wet cavities. Insulation, drywall or cabinet pieces beyond saving come out, and moldy material is bagged to keep spores from spreading through the house.

Structural Drying

We keep dehumidifiers and air movers going until the subfloor and framing read normal, which usually takes 3 to 5 days. We check readings on each visit and adjust the equipment as materials dry.

Final Treatment and Records

Affected surfaces get an antimicrobial treatment, and you receive photos along with the moisture logs. Those records show the area was dry before repairs and help with any claim.
