Maintenance Tips for Mold Remediation

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Check that exhaust fans in the kitchen, laundry room and bathrooms move enough air
  • Regular filter changes and HVAC maintenance help keep mold out of the ducts
  • Use mold-resistant drywall and paint in moisture-prone areas like bathrooms and basements
  • Clear gutters and downspouts regularly, which keeps rain from soaking the foundation walls
  • Fix water leaks within 24 hours, because mold needs only moisture and 24 to 48 hours to start growing
  • Use ventilation and a dehumidifier to hold humidity under 60%, with 30 to 50% as the ideal

Signs It Is Time for Mold Remediation

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Persistent musty or earthy odors, especially in enclosed or humid areas
  • Cleaning that never lasts, with growth returning to the same surface
  • Furniture against outside walls or items in closets developing speckled patches
  • Signs of concealed moisture such as paint that bubbles, wallpaper that peels or materials that warp
  • A flood or leak in the recent past that never got a professional dry-out
  • Headaches, allergy flare-ups or breathing symptoms that ease whenever you leave the house
  • Windows, cold surfaces or walls that sweat with condensation on a regular basis

Why Hire a Professional for Mold Remediation?

Mold remediation is not a DIY project. Without containment, scrubbing or tearing out moldy material releases spores by the millions, potentially spreading contamination through the house and making the health exposure worse than it was. Capturing and containing the spores during removal takes HEPA-filtered scrubbers, protective equipment for the crew and containment kept under negative air pressure, which is how professionals do it.

A professional job goes after the moisture source too, because that is why the mold grew in the first place. Clean off the visible growth but leave the moisture problem, and the mold comes back, in many cases in a matter of weeks. Finding concealed moisture, drying it properly and preventing a repeat all take equipment and training, which certified remediation technicians have.
